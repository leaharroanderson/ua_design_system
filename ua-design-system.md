## Color System

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| **Background** | Bone | `#f6f5f0` | Default page background, light surfaces, email body |
| **Surface Alt** | Cream | `#eeece3` | Section bands, cards, subtle separation |
| **Text / Dark** | Near-black | `#0a0a0a` | Body text, headings, dark cards, footer |
| **Primary Action** | Lime | `#ccff00` | CTA buttons, highlights, accent lines—NEVER text |
| **White** | Card White | `#ffffff` | Cards, popovers, high-contrast surfaces |

### Color Rules
- **Lime is a button/border color only.** Never set body copy in lime. Never use for small labels.
- **On lime backgrounds:** Text is always Near-black.
- **On dark backgrounds:** Text is Bone/Cream at 70–100% opacity for hierarchy.
- **Borders:** Near-black at 12% opacity (default) or 30% (strong emphasis).

## Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Size | Notes |
|------|------|--------|------|-------|
| **Wordmark** | Archivo Black / Inter Light | 400 | Display | "Urban" in Archivo Black; "Athletics" in Inter Light; both uppercase |
| **Display Headings** | Oswald | 700 | 48–60px | Uppercase, line-height 0.95, letter-spacing -0.02em |
| **Body** | Inter | 300 | 16px | Light weight, leading-relaxed (~1.625) |
| **Labels/Eyebrows** | Inter | 600 | 11px (0.6875rem) | Uppercase, very wide tracking (0.22em) |
| **Buttons** | Inter | 600 | 12px (0.75rem) | Uppercase, tracking 0.18em, height 48px minimum |

### Heading Treatment
- H1–H3: Oswald 700, uppercase, line-height 0.95, letter-spacing -0.02em
- Never sentence-case headings
- Large sizes (5xl–6xl / 48–60px on desktop); scale down on mobile

### Body Treatment
- Light weight (300), generous line-height (≥1.625), Near-black at full strength
- Secondary/muted text: Near-black at 50–70% opacity

## Logo & Wordmark

```
Urban Athletics
```

- **"Urban"** — Archivo Black, uppercase, tracking-tight, Near-black (or Cream on dark)
- **"Athletics"** — Inter Light, uppercase, same color, tight spacing
- **No separate icon or swoosh.** Text-only wordmark.
- **Minimum clear space:** Equal to the cap-height of "Urban" on all sides
- Do NOT stretch, outline, or change typeface pairing

## UI Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (CTA):** Lime bg (`#ccff00`), Near-black text, uppercase, 12px, tracking 0.18em, height 48px, padding 28px horizontal
- **Ghost (light bg):** Transparent, 1px Near-black/30 border, Near-black text
- **Ghost (dark bg):** Transparent, 1px Cream/40 border, Cream text
- **Hover state:** Slight lift (`translateY(-3px)`) + soft shadow
- **Corner radius:** 0.25rem (4px base)

### Cards
- White or Near-black background
- 1px border at 12% opacity
- Padding: 32px standard
- Hover: lift shadow on interactive cards

### Links & Navigation
- Desktop nav: uppercase, 12px, tracking 0.18em, lime underline (animates in on hover)
- Active nav item: lime text

### Eyebrows
- Small uppercase label above heading
- 11px, uppercase, tracking 0.22em, semibold
- Color: muted foreground (50–70% opacity)

## Voice & Messaging

**Tone:**
- Direct, physical, confident
- Short sentences. Active verbs.
- No hype, no empty superlatives ("best," "#1")
- No unverifiable stats or claims

**Sample Phrases:**
- "Train. Recover. Optimize."
- "Houston's first gym-as-clinic model."
- "One of the closest gyms to Downtown Houston."
- "Find your spot on the floor."
- "Know when to show up."
- "No membership. No problem."

**Avoid:**
- Wellness clichés: "holistic," "mind-body," "find your balance"
- Bootcamp clichés: "crush," "beast mode," "no excuses"
- Unverifiable claims about results, rankings, medical outcomes

## Photography Direction

**Use ONLY real Urban Athletics photos.** No stock gym imagery. No AI-generated fitness scenes.

**Preferred subjects:**
- Members training inside gym or outdoor turf
- HYROX sessions, sled pushes, rope pulls, rowers, kettlebell work
- Coaches interacting with members
- Community events and group shots
- UA wall logo / branded interior (when natural)

**Lighting / Mood:**
- Moody, high-contrast, athletic
- Dark gym interiors with focused light; outdoor turf with strong sunlight
- Avoid overly bright, airy, wellness-spa aesthetics

**Cropping:**
- Leave room for headline overlays (left or center-left)
- Subject matter off to one side so text can sit over quiet areas
- Real effort and focus, not generic posed smiles

## Key Do's & Don'ts

| ✓ Do | ✗ Don't |
|-----|--------|
| Use Bone (`#f6f5f0`) as default background | Use pure white `#ffffff` as page background |
| Use Lime for CTA buttons and accents | Use Lime for body text or small labels |
| Set headings in Oswald 700 uppercase | Use sentence-case or serif headings |
| Use real gym photos from UA library | Use stock fitness photos or AI-generated scenes |
| Keep copy short and action-oriented | Write long paragraphs or wellness jargon |
| Use "Train. Recover. Optimize." | Invent new taglines without approval |
| Keep wordmark as Archivo Black + Inter Light | Add icons, outlines, or effects to wordmark |

## Campaign-Specific Guidance

### Email Design
- Use Bone background as default
- Lime for primary CTA buttons (48px height minimum, touch-friendly)
- Inter body copy (300 weight, relaxed leading)
- Oswald for subject lines and section headers (uppercase)
- Single accent line (Lime) above key sections
- Mobile-first design (stack vertically, large touch targets)

### Landing Pages
- Bone background with Cream section breaks
- Hero image with left/center-left text overlay (high contrast)
- Multiple Lime CTAs (hero, mid-page, footer)
- Use real UA photos only
- Near-black body copy, Oswald headings

### Dashboard / UI
- Bone background, white cards
- Near-black text hierarchy (100% → 70% → 50% opacity)
- Lime for active states, badges, status indicators
- Clean borders (Near-black at 12% opacity)
- Touch-friendly interactive elements (48px minimum)
